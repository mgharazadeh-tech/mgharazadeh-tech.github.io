Mehrdad Gharazadeh

Mechanical Engineer · Storage Tanks, Pressure Vessels, CAD

I have about 10 years of experience with static equipment for refineries, gas condensate and petrochemical plants in Iran. I design and draw API 650 storage tanks from 6 m to 94 m in diameter, with all common roof types: fixed cone and dome roofs (self-supported, on rafters and columns, or on girders without columns), external double-deck and pontoon floating roofs, and internal floating roofs. I also design ASME VIII pressure vessels, such as vertical air receivers and horizontal process vessels, and TEMA shell-and-tube heat exchangers with U-tubes.

My work covers the whole job: mechanical calculation, general arrangement and fabrication drawings, plate cutting plans, material take-off and the full vendor document package. Clients usually approved my drawings at revision 2 or 3. Now I am finishing my M.Sc. at TU Chemnitz, where I work with FEA in Abaqus. In one study my pulley design lowered the peak contact pressure by 78 %.

R-01

Less contact pressure between a braided rope and its pulley

Synthetic ropes wear where they press hardest on the pulley. I built a 12-strand braided rope with Python in Grasshopper, modelled four pulley surfaces and compared the peak contact pressure in Abaqus/Explicit. A wavy surface that follows the braid at 45° gave the lowest pressure.

Peak contact pressure, CPRESS (MPa)
Flat (reference)4.519
Grooved2.519
Wavy 30°4.810
Wavy 45°, matched0.980
Solver
Abaqus/Explicit, C3D8R, general contact
Rope
6.5 mm, 12 strands, 40 mm pitch
Pulley
Steel, Johnson–Cook plasticity
Result
78 % lower peak pressure than flat
R-02

Master's thesis: burrs in deformational cutting of PA6

When polyamide 6 is cut by deformation, burrs form and then spring back as the material recovers and gets soft from heat. Current Abaqus models do not show this well. My thesis models how heating or cooling the tool changes the burr.

  • Abaqus/Explicit with Lagrangian and Coupled Eulerian–Lagrangian (CEL) models.
  • Improving the PA6 VUMAT user material for damage and viscoplastic recovery.
  • Python scripts that read residual stress and temperature from the results.
